Automation & Event Triggers

Kathmandu Valley Secondary School • Real-time SMS Triggers, Nightly Crons & Scheduled Dispatches

Automated Absent Parent SMS (Daily Roll Call)

Active Trigger

When a student is marked Absent in the 09:30 AM roll call, an instant SMS alert is dispatched to the guardian's verified phone via Sparrow SMS (Sender ID: KVSS_EDU).

Monthly Fee Due Reminder Scheduler (12th of Month)

Active Trigger

Sends a friendly payment link with eSewa and Khalti integration on the 12th of each Nepali month before the 15th due date to avoid 5% late fee surcharge.

Student & Staff Birthday Greetings

Active Trigger

Dispatches a personalized celebratory greeting in Nepali/English to student and parents at 08:00 AM on their recorded birthday.

Official Candidate Instructions (NEB Code of Conduct)

  • Candidates must produce their signed Examination Admit Card at the entrance of the examination hall.
  • Entry will close precisely 15 minutes after paper commencement (11:15 AM). No extra time will be allotted for late arrival.
  • Electronic devices including smartwatches, cellular phones, and programmable calculators are strictly prohibited. Non-programmable scientific calculators are permitted for Mathematics & Science papers.
  • Pass marks threshold: A minimum of 35% (27 out of 75 marks) in theory is mandatory to prevent Non-Graded (NG) classification under CDC letter grading regulations.

Academic / Examination 20 Bhadra 2083 (05 Sep 2026)

Schedule for First Terminal Examination & SEE Model Test 2083

The First Terminal Examination for Classes 1 to 12 commences on 28th Ashwin 2083. Class 10 candidates will appear for SEE mock blueprint papers under full National Examination Board protocol. Full marks: 75 Theory + 25 Practical. Admit cards are available in the student portal.

Published by: Examination Controller

KVSS is proud to host 28 Valley schools for the Drone Simulator, Line Follower Robotics, and AI Innovation Arena on 12th Ashwin 2083. Students interested in entering technical prototypes should register with Mr. Bikram Gurung by 25th Bhadra.

Published by: IT & Robotics Club

Quarter 2 Fee Settlement Notice & eSewa/Khalti Online Clearance

Parents who have pending fee dues for Quarter 2 are kindly requested to clear them before 30th Bhadra. Online payments via eSewa, Khalti, and ConnectIPS are available with instant digital receipt generation.

The school will remain closed for the auspicious festivals of Bada Dashain, Tihar, and Chhath from Ashwin 29 to Kartik 18, 2083. Regular classes resume on Kartik 19 (Sunday). Holiday homework has been dispatched.
